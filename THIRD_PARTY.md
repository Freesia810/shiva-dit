# Third-party code

[`diffusers/`](diffusers/) contains a subset of Hugging Face Diffusers, modified for Shiva-DiT's hook integration. The source files retain their upstream copyright headers and license notices. Its Apache 2.0 license is included in [`diffusers/LICENSE`](diffusers/LICENSE). [Upstream project](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers)

[`baselines/`](baselines/) contains adapters or reimplementations of the methods cited in the paper. The source and associated papers are listed in the [baseline guide](baselines/README.md).

Base models, datasets, and metric checkpoints come from their respective providers and are governed by the corresponding terms. The [model and data table](README.md#models-and-data) links to their source pages.
